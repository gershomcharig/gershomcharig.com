This is a **demo post** for customising the Notes template. Keep it open while adjusting type, spacing, and colour, then replace it with your own writing.

A short paragraph gives the page a little breathing room.

A longer paragraph helps you judge the reading measure. Good defaults should make both a quick observation and a more involved explanation feel comfortable. Look at how the lines wrap, how much space sits between paragraphs, and whether the text still feels easy to follow on a narrow screen.

## Emphasis and inline elements

Try **bold text**, *italic text*, and ***both together***. Here is a [link to the portfolio](/), a [link to another note](/notes/demo-images-and-media/), and some inline code: `npm run dev`.

You can also use HTML for <mark>highlighted text</mark>, <del>deleted text</del>, <small>a small aside</small>, H<sub>2</sub>O, and x<sup>2</sup>. An abbreviation such as <abbr title="User experience">UX</abbr> can carry a little more context.
